# Limitations

Written 2026-10-18, after the window closed and after the first full
measurement, before the unpredicted pool was named. Recorded here rather than
left for a reader to find.

## The window is not a typical week

Stated by the subject on reading the first result:

> I think I was off since we had 2 days off from school this last week. So if
> I'm not at class, not talking to many people.

Two of the eight days in the window were school holidays. That is a real
constraint on generalizing from these shares to a normal week.

It does not rescue the result, and the direction is worth stating plainly:

- School measured 19.1% against 10% predicted. If the holidays suppressed
  any category it was that one, and it still came in at nearly double.
- The three stated priorities (finish the hackathon builds, finish the ACSM
  abstract, finish more Handshake tasks) are not classroom activities. Days
  away from class are precisely the discretionary hours those three compete
  for. They measured 5.4% combined. Warzone measured 33.5% on the same hours.

A week with two unplanned free days is the best chance a stated priority
gets. The free hours went elsewhere.

## Capture is not uniform across settings

36.7% of captured audio in the window is classified as the wearer's own
participation. The remaining 1,233 minutes are media, ambient, or unclear.

Capture is also uneven across places. The wearer carries the device by
choice, and during this window carried it at home more than away from it.
Topics that happen at home are better represented than topics that happen
elsewhere. This is a property of the instrument. No correction is applied for
it, because any correction would require knowing the unobserved time, which
is the thing the instrument cannot see.

## Not measured

- **Silent work.** Bee observes conversation. An hour spent writing the ACSM
  abstract alone produces no conversational evidence and is not counted
  anywhere. These shares describe conversational focus and nothing else. They
  are not a claim about how the week was spent.
- **Unassigned own time.** 135.8 minutes of own speech, 19.0% of the window,
  carry no identifiable subject: greetings, logistics, fragments. That time
  stays in the denominator rather than being redistributed, which deflates
  every topic share. The alternative, forcing each utterance into a topic,
  would inflate all of them. On attributed time alone the ordering is
  unchanged: Warzone 41.3%, School 23.4%, the three stated priorities 6.6%.
- **Filter accuracy.** The own, media, ambient and unclear classification has
  no measured disagreement rate yet. `docs/denominator.md` commits to
  publishing one. That commitment is outstanding at the time of writing.
