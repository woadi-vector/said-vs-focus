    How to read this

    Shares are computed over conversation the wearer took part in, after the media filter, as fixed in docs/denominator.md before the window closed. Television is excluded from the denominator and is not eligible to be a topic. The second control switches to everything captured, published as a sensitivity check so the denominator cannot be accused of flattering the prediction.

    Topic durations hold to roughly five percent between identical runs, but topics within about a minute of each other swap places, so a ranking between two near-tied rows is not a measurement. docs/variance.md fixes the procedure. Minutes are the sum of utterance durations, not wall clock.

    Every row opens to the segments behind it, the filter's own reason for calling each one yours, and the lines it was built from. Marking a segment as not yours removes it from the denominator and from every topic it fed, and the ranking recomputes.
